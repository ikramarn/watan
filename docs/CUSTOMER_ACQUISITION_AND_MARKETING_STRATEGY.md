# GetChum Customer Acquisition & Marketing Strategy Blueprint
**Focus:** Passenger Demand Generation & Scalable Local Growth  
**Operational Market:** Fife & Edinburgh, Scotland  
**Core Thesis:** Solving the Two-Sided Marketplace Cold-Start Problem without Large Advertising Budgets  

---

## 1. The Core Challenge: Supply vs. Demand

In on-demand transportation, supply (drivers) can be onboarded quickly with favorable economics (such as GetChum's 8% platform fee versus Uber's 25% or traditional £140/week radio rent). 

**The critical bottleneck is passenger demand:**
* Generic social media ads across broad geographies burn capital with high cost-per-acquisition (CPA) and low retention.
* Spreading limited driver supply over a wide area leads to 25-minute wait times, resulting in booking cancellations and app uninstalls.
* **The Solution:** A high-density, corridor-focused organic acquisition engine that turns active drivers, local hospitality venues, and commuter hubs into zero-cost marketing channels.

## 2. Pillar 1: The In-Car Driver Referral Engine

Drivers already have dozens of paying passengers sitting in their vehicles every day. In traditional setups, drivers have no incentive to promote an app because they pay flat rent regardless. 

Under GetChum's 8% fee / £35 cap model, **drivers earn significantly more when rides are booked through GetChum** rather than street-hailed or booked through high-commission platforms.

### Execution:
1. **Physical In-Car Assets:**
   * Equip each onboarded driver with professional headrest display cards or dashboard flyers:
     > *"Book your next journey directly on GetChum. Get £3 off your first ride. Scan to book instantly."*
2. **Personalized Driver QR Codes:**
   * Each driver receives a unique referral QR code linked to their profile.
   * When a passenger scans the code:
     * The **passenger** gets £3.00 off their first journey.
     * The **driver** receives a £1.00 credit toward their weekly platform fee cap.
3. **Economics:**
   * If 15 active drivers convert just 2 passengers per day, the platform acquires **900 active local customers in Month 1** at near-zero ad spend.

## 3. Pillar 2: Hyper-Dense Corridor Focus (Density over Dispersion)

A key operational pitfall is attempting to cover all of Fife and Edinburgh on Day 1. Instead of spreading 15 drivers across 40 square miles, concentrate supply on **high-frequency, short-distance transit corridors** where 3–5 minute pickup times can be guaranteed.

### Priority Corridors:

#### A. St Andrews $\longleftrightarrow$ Leuchars Railway Station
* **Audience:** 10,000+ university students, academic staff, and international visitors.
* **Problem:** St Andrews has no direct rail link; the 5-mile run to Leuchars station is mandatory. Existing local cab companies frequently quote 40+ minute waits on weekends.
* **GetChum Advantage:** Reliable, scheduled 5-minute runs with upfront fixed pricing.

#### B. Fife Commuter Hubs $\longleftrightarrow$ Edinburgh Airport (EDI)
* **Audience:** Dunfermline, Kirkcaldy, and Inverkeithing business commuters and vacationers.
* **Problem:** High-cost parking at EDI (£40–£90) and unreliable early-morning airport transfers.
* **GetChum Advantage:** Fixed-price pre-booked transfers (£45–£65). Pre-booking eliminates the need to keep idle cars waiting on standby.

#### C. Weekend Night Economy Bridge (Edinburgh $\longleftrightarrow$ Fife)
* Late-night weekend routes where ride-hailing demand surges 2x–3x in Edinburgh city centre, leaving Fife-bound passengers stranded.

## 4. Pillar 3: B2B Hospitality & Venue QR Terminals

Venues and hospitality staff frequently deal with guests needing transport where phone-in taxi bases fail to answer or have extended delays.

### Execution:
1. **Zero-Friction Web Booking:**
   * Place branded acrylic QR code stands on reception desks, concierge counters, and bar areas:
     > *"Need a ride to Edinburgh Airport or the station? Scan to book instantly on GetChum."*
   * Scanning the QR code opens the lightweight GetChum passenger web app (`https://getchum.co.uk`) directly in the browser—**no app store download required**.
2. **Target Venues:**
   * Independent hotels, boutique guest houses, and B&Bs in Fife and Edinburgh.
   * Wedding venues, golf clubhouses (e.g., St Andrews, Kingsbarns, Crail), and late-night food/pub venues.
3. **Venue Incentive:**
   * Offer venues a 50p booking kickback per completed trip, or position it as a premium complimentary guest convenience.

---

## 5. Pillar 4: High-Intent Organic Local SEO

Passengers booking airport and intercity transfers search Google using specific long-tail keywords rather than searching for app brands.

### Execution:
* Create dedicated, lightweight SEO landing pages on `getchum.co.uk` targeting high-intent search queries:
  * `/taxi/dunfermline-to-edinburgh-airport`
  * `/taxi/st-andrews-to-edinburgh-airport`
  * `/taxi/kirkcaldy-to-edinburgh-airport`
  * `/taxi/leuchars-to-st-andrews`
* Each page features an interactive instant quote calculator connecting directly to GetChum’s pricing backend, converting search traffic into immediate pre-booked rides.

## 6. Pillar 5: Student Union & Campus Partnerships

* **Target:** University of St Andrews Students' Association, Heriot-Watt University, and Edinburgh Napier campuses.
* **The "Safe Ride Home" Campaign:**
  * Pitch GetChum as the reliable, community-first safety app for students traveling late at night.
  * Key selling points: Live GPS trip tracking shareable with friends, transparent fixed pricing (no 3x surge multipliers at 2 AM), and vetted licensed drivers.
  * Launch student-specific discount codes (e.g., `STUDENTCHUM`) distributed via student society social channels and freshers' packs.
